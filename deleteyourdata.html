Delete Your Data

November 2023

If you'd like to delete all data we maintain related to your use of SeeSpotBid (Search Alerts, paid subscriptions, and email accounts), please follow the instructions on this page.

In the App

If you still have SeeSpotBid installed on your phone, you can initiate the deletion of all your user data from the app. Navigate to the Settings page and find the "Delete My Data" button in the Account section. It will walk you through the steps required.

Email Us

If you don't have the app installed, you can email us and we will initiate the data deletion protocol from our end.
